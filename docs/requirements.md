---

This version requires **PHP 8.4+** and **Laravel 12 or higher**.

If you're on an older version of PHP or Laravel, take a look at [v4](https://spatie.be/docs/laravel-activitylog/v4), [v3](https://spatie.be/docs/laravel-activitylog/v3), [v2](https://spatie.be/docs/laravel-activitylog/v2) or [v1](https://spatie.be/docs/laravel-activitylog/v1) of this package.
